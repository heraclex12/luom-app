Lượm được từ mới wherever you find them.

Select an English word in any app, press one shortcut, and it is saved with its Vietnamese meaning, pronunciation and examples. Then keep it for good with a few minutes of review and games made from your own words.

How it works

From a word you meet to a word you own.

01

Pick it up

Reading an article, a PDF or a chat? Select the word and press ⌥ ⌘ E. A small card shows the Vietnamese meaning and saves it.

02

Review a little

Spaced repetition brings each word back right before you would forget it. Most days take five minutes.

03

Play to remember

Every answer in the games counts as a review, and your garden grows as words stick.

Games

Cute games, made from your words.

Your due words come first, so playing is reviewing. Each game trains a different kind of memory: spelling, listening, recall and speed.

Bubble Tea Shop Spelling

Animal customers order by meaning. Each right letter drops a pearl into the cup; patient customers tip more.

Word Fishing Recall

Catch the fish carrying the right word.

Firefly Night Listening

Hear a word, catch the firefly with its spelling. Your jar glows brighter.

Frog Hop Speed

Hop onto the right meaning. Quick answers become spinning leaps.

Word Bridge Spelling

Type the word letter by letter to build a bridge.

Your aquarium

Every word you catch in Word Fishing comes to live here. Fish grow as you learn their words, and the ones you master turn golden.

Daily episodes

A story that only continues if you do.

Each day brings a new episode of a story written around the words you are learning, ending on a cliffhanger. Tap any word for its meaning, or reveal the Vietnamese line by line.

  • Fourteen episodes a season, in the genre you pick.
  • Miss a day and that page is lost for good.
  • A short quiz after each episode keeps the words fresh.

Everything else

Small touches that keep you going.

Read in the app

EPUB, PDF and Markdown. Select a word while reading to save it or hear it.

AI built in, free

Better examples and explanations with one click. Works out of the box, or with your ChatGPT account.

Gentle reminders

A daily nudge and tiny pop quiz cards in the corner of your screen.

Natural voices

Hear every word and example in US or UK English.

Private by design

No account. Your words stay on your Mac.

Updates itself

New versions install quietly in the background.

Install

Up and running in a minute.

Lượm is a free app from an independent developer and isn't sold through the App Store, so macOS asks you to confirm the first time you open it.

Download for Mac
  1. Open the downloaded fileDouble-click Luom-arm64.dmg and drag Lượm into the Applications folder.
  2. Open LượmmacOS says it can't check the app. Click Done.
  3. Allow it onceOpen System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Lượm. You only do this once.
  4. Let it read your selectionFor the ⌥ ⌘ E shortcut, allow Lượm in Privacy & Security → Accessibility when asked.

Needs a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 12 or later. Older versions are on the releases page.
