Up and running
in a minute.

The first time you open Lượm, macOS asks you to confirm. Here is how.

Open the download

Double-click the file and drag Lượm into Applications.

Open Lượm

macOS says it can’t check the app. Click Done.

Allow it once

In System Settings, open Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

Turn on the shortcut

Allow Lượm under Accessibility so ⌥⌘E can read your selection.

Needs a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 12 or later.
