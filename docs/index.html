From a word you meet to a word you own.

Pick it up.

Reading an article, a PDF or a chat? Select the word and press ⌥ ⌘ E. A small card shows the Vietnamese meaning, the pronunciation and an example, and saves it.

Review a little.

Spaced repetition brings each word back right before you would forget it. Most days it takes about five minutes.

Play to remember.

Every answer in the games counts as a review, and your 3D garden grows as the words stick.

Cute games, made from your own words.

Install

Up and running in a minute.

Lượm comes from an independent developer, not the App Store, so macOS asks you to confirm the first time you open it.

Needs a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 12 or later.

  1. Open the downloadDouble-click Luom-arm64.dmg and drag Lượm into Applications.
  2. Open LượmmacOS says it can’t check the app. Click Done.
  3. Allow it onceIn System Settings, open Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Lượm.
  4. Let it read your selectionFor the ⌥ ⌘ E shortcut, allow Lượm under Privacy & Security, then Accessibility, when it asks.

Start keeping the words you meet.
