Privacy

Your words stay
on your Mac.

Last updated 15 October 2026

The short version

  • There is no account. Your words, progress, notes, books and writing are saved only on your Mac.
  • Some features work online: looking up a word, pronunciation, translation and AI. Each sends only the word or text that request needs.
  • Lượm collects a few anonymous usage stats, such as how AI features are used, to make the app better. Never your words or anything you type, and you can turn it off.
  • No ads, nothing sold. Lượm is free and its source code is public.

What stays on your Mac

Everything you build in Lượm is kept on your Mac: the words you save, their meanings, your learning progress, notes, word collections, the books you import, highlights and reading progress, sentences you write in Write back, your Daily Episodes, and your settings. There is no Lượm account and no cloud copy. You can export your words as a CSV file any time from Settings → Data & about.

  • Say it: your voice is turned into text by Apple’s speech recognition on your Mac, and the recording is deleted right after. It never leaves your Mac.
  • Quick capture: with the Accessibility permission, Lượm reads the text you have selected only when you press your capture shortcut. If nothing is selected, it uses what you last copied.
  • Desktop widget: it shows your words from your Mac. Nothing is sent anywhere.

What leaves your Mac, and why

Some features need an online service. Each one gets only what it needs for that one request.

  • Looking up a word

    The word or phrase, and its English definitions and examples to translate into Vietnamese.

    Dictionary and translation services such as Google Translate and Wiktionary.

  • Translating in the reader

    The sentence or passage you ask to translate.

    Google Translate or Microsoft’s translator.

  • Pronunciation and reading aloud

    The word or sentence to be spoken. The audio is then kept on your Mac, so each word is fetched only once.

    Microsoft Edge’s online voices, or the macOS voice on your Mac when they are unavailable.

  • AI features

    What the request needs: for example the word to explain, your words for a story or episode, or the sentence you wrote for feedback.

    Lượm’s free AI, or the AI service you choose in Settings → AI.

  • Updates

    A check for a newer version, and the download when there is one.

    GitHub, where Lượm is published.

Outside services handle each request under their own privacy policies. Lượm sends them nothing about you beyond the request itself.

AI features

You choose where AI requests go, in Settings → AI:

  • Lượm (Free) is free AI provided by Lượm, ready to use with no account or key.
  • Your own AI account sends requests to that account, under that provider’s terms.
  • A custom API sends requests to the service you set up. Your API key is stored encrypted on your Mac.

AI answers are made online, so please leave private details, like passwords or personal information, out of what you write for AI feedback.

Anonymous usage stats

To make Lượm better, it collects a few anonymous usage stats, such as how AI features are used and whether they work well. They are not linked to you. You can turn them off any time in Settings → Data & about.

Lượm never collects:

  • your words, their meanings, or your learning progress;
  • sentences you write, or anything you type or select;
  • your books, highlights or notes;
  • recordings of your voice;
  • your name, email or contacts.

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics or ads.

The community board. When you post, we keep your message and, if you add them, your name and email. Public posts are read before they appear. Your email is never shown; it is only used to reply to you. Private posts go only to the developer.

What Lượm never does

  • Show ads, or sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • Ask you to create an account, or upload your words or books.
  • Use the camera, your location or your contacts.
  • Listen through the microphone outside Say it, or read selected text without your shortcut.

Your choices

  • Turn off usage stats in Settings → Data & about.
  • Choose which AI service to use in Settings → AI, or don’t use AI features at all.
  • Remove the Accessibility or microphone permission any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  • To remove a post or an email you sent through the board, just ask.
